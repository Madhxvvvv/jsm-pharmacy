01 · The course

Two years of study, then supervised practice

The D.Pharm at JSM College of Pharmacy follows the Pharmacy Council of India's Education Regulations 2020. After the two academic years, students complete practical training before they are eligible to register as a pharmacist.

2 yrsPart I and Part II, one academic year each
500 hrsPractical training after the two years
10+2Science stream entry, as set by PCI
60Current PCI cap on D.Pharm intake per institution

Part IYear one

  • Pharmaceutics theory + lab
  • Pharmaceutical Chemistry theory + lab
  • Pharmacognosy theory + lab
  • Human Anatomy & Physiology theory + lab
  • Social Pharmacy theory + lab

Part IIYear two

  • Pharmacology theory + lab
  • Community Pharmacy & Management theory + lab
  • Biochemistry & Clinical Pathology theory + lab
  • Pharmacotherapeutics theory
  • Hospital & Clinical Pharmacy theory
  • Pharmacy Law & Ethics theory

Students also sit the Diploma in Pharmacy Exit Examination introduced by PCI from the 2022-23 batch. Check the current PCI regulations for the latest rules on eligibility, intake and examinations.

02 · Labs and practice rooms

Six places where the theory gets used

Every theory subject in the D.Pharm has a practical component. This is what each room is for and what students do in it.

LAB 01

Pharmaceutics Lab

Compounding and dosage forms. Students prepare and label what a pharmacist makes up from a prescription.

syrupssuspensionsemulsionsointmentspowders
LAB 02

Pharmaceutical Chemistry Lab

Titration, limit tests for impurities and assay of common drugs, done on a burette stand with measured reagents.

volumetric analysislimit testsassay
LAB 03

Pharmacognosy Lab

Drugs from plants. Students identify crude drugs by appearance, microscopy and simple chemical tests.

crude drug identificationmicroscopypowder analysis
LAB 04

Human Anatomy & Physiology Lab

Models, charts and prepared slides for the body systems, plus measurements students take on each other.

anatomical modelshistology slidespulse & BPblood grouping
LAB 05

Biochemistry & Clinical Pathology Lab

Routine urine and blood tests of the kind run in a clinical lab, with attention to what each result means.

urine analysisblood glucosehaemoglobin
LAB 06

Model Pharmacy

A mock community pharmacy for practising prescription reading, dispensing, labelling, patient counselling and record keeping.

dispensingcounsellingrecordsSchedule H drugs

04 · After the diploma

Work now, or keep studying

A D.Pharm from a PCI-approved institution, plus the required practical training, qualifies a student to register as a pharmacist with a State Pharmacy Council. Graduates work in:

Community pharmacy

Dispensing and counselling at retail chemists.

Hospital pharmacy

Stores, dispensing and ward supply.

Manufacturing

Production, packing and quality checks.

Distribution & sales

Wholesale, stockists and medical representation.

Pharmacy education ladder in India

Private pharmacy colleges

Standalone colleges offering D.Pharm and B.Pharm under a university or board.

Diploma institutions

Approved by PCI under section 12 of the Pharmacy Act, 1948 to run D.Pharm.

Before joining any pharmacy college, check that it appears in the PCI approved-institutions list at pci.nic.in.
